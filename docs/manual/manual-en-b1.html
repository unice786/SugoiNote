Part 1 · Person 1

Your Account and the Home Page

This part explains what SugoiNote is, how the system is organized, and how to create your account, log in, recover your password, and log out safely.

In this part you will learn

  • What SugoiNote is and which layers it has.
  • How to create an account and log in.
  • How to recover your password and log out safely.

1. What is SugoiNote?

SugoiNote is a web system for students. It puts five tools in one place: your notes, AI summaries, AI exams, an AI chat, and a community of students. You do not install a program. You open a browser, log in, and work.

2. The three layers of the system

LayerWhat it doesExample
Web (frontend)The pages that you see and click.Menus, buttons, the note editor.
Backend / APIThe logic, the security rules, and the data.ASP.NET Core, the services, SQL Server.
Mobile appThe same system on a phone.Expo / React Native.

The web uses a session cookie. The mobile app uses a JWT token. Both use the same backend, so the behaviour is the same.

3. The side menu

Menu itemWhat you can do there
InicioSee your recent notes and create a new one.
Mis ApuntesSee, filter, open, and delete your notes.
Mis ResúmenesGenerate and read AI summaries.
ExámenesGenerate exams, answer them, and see your grade.
Chat IAAsk the AI tutor questions and send images.
ComunidadAsk questions, create polls, answer, and vote.
PerfilChange your data and photo, and see your statistics.

4. Create your account

Step by step

  1. First, open the web address in your browser. In development it is http://localhost:5199.
  2. Next, click “Regístrate aquí” under the login form.
  3. Then, write your full name, your email, and a password.
  4. After that, repeat the password in the second field.
  5. Finally, click “Registrarme”. The system creates your account.

The password needs 8 characters or more, with at least one letter and one number.

5. Log in and log out

Step by step

  1. First, write your email and your password.
  2. Next, click “Iniciar sesión”.
  3. Then, the home page opens with your name and your recent notes.
  4. Finally, to leave, click “Cerrar sesión” at the bottom of the side menu.

After 5 wrong attempts, the system locks your account for 10 minutes. This security rule is called a lockout.

6. Recover your password

Step by step

  1. First, click “¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?”.
  2. Next, write your email and click “Enviar enlace”.
  3. Then, open the email and click the link.
  4. After that, write and confirm your new password.
  5. Finally, log in again with the new password.

The link works only one time. In development, the email is not sent: the link appears in the application log.

Part 2 · Person 2

Notes and the Editor

This part explains how to create, write, organize, import, export, and delete your notes in the SugoiNote editor.

In this part you will learn

  • Where your notes are and how to create a new one.
  • How to use the editor toolbar and the autosave.
  • How to import a document, export a copy, and delete a note.

1. Home and “Mis Apuntes”

On Inicio you see your name and your recent notes (maximum 8). Click a card to open it, or click “Nueva Nota” to start. The page Mis Apuntes shows all your notes. You can filter them by subject with the pills at the top: “Todas” and one pill for each subject.

2. Create a note

Step by step

  1. First, click “Nueva Nota”.
  2. Next, write a title for the note.
  3. Then, choose a subject from the list, or write a new subject name in “O crea una nueva materia”.
  4. After that, click “Crear nota”.
  5. Finally, the editor opens and you can write your content.

A subject (in Spanish, materia) is a group of notes, for example “Cálculo” or “Historia”. The system can create the subject at the same time as the note.

3. The editor toolbar

Bold / Italic / UnderlineChange the style of the selected text.
H2 / H3Create headings to organize the note.
ListsCreate a bullet list or a numbered list.
LinkAdd a web link to the text.
“Insertar” menuAdd a table, an image, two or three columns, a checklist, a code block, a date, a horizontal rule, or a page break.
“Subir documento”Import a PDF, DOCX, HTML, or TXT file.
“Descargar”Export the note as TXT, PDF, or Word.

4. Autosave and “Guardar”

The editor saves your work automatically while you write. The small badge at the top shows the state: “Sin cambios” means there is nothing new to save. You can also click “Guardar” to save immediately. Always check the badge before you close the page.

5. Import and export

Use “Subir documento” to bring a file into the editor. The system accepts PDF, DOCX, HTML, and TXT. For images, open the “Insertar” menu and choose “Imagen”; the accepted formats are PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WEBP.

Use “Descargar” to keep a copy of your note. You can choose TXT (plain text), PDF (to print or save), or Word (a .doc file).

6. Delete a note

Step by step

  1. First, open “Mis Apuntes”.
  2. Next, click “Eliminar” to activate the delete mode.
  3. Then, click the trash icon on the note you want to remove.
  4. After that, read the confirmation message.
  5. Finally, click “Sí, eliminar”. This action cannot be undone.

Before the system saves the note, it sanitizes the HTML. This means it removes dangerous code, such as <script>, to protect all users.

Part 3 · Person 3

AI Summaries and AI Exams

This part explains how to generate a summary from a note, how to create an exam with artificial intelligence, and how to understand your grade.

In this part you will learn

  • How to generate and download an AI summary.
  • How to generate an exam and answer it.
  • How to read your grade and the explanations.

1. What the AI can do

The system uses artificial intelligence (AI) to read your notes and create new study material. It can write a summary, create an exam, and answer questions in the chat. The AI works with a model: you send a prompt (an instruction) and the model sends a response.

2. Generate a summary

Step by step

  1. First, open “Mis Resúmenes”.
  2. Next, click “Generar Resumen”.
  3. Then, select one of your notes from the list.
  4. After that, click “Generar”.
  5. Finally, wait. The AI reads the note and writes the summary.

A long summary can take more than one minute. If the note is empty, the summary can be empty too. In that case, write more content in the note and try again.

3. Read and download the summary

When the AI finishes, the summary opens in a panel. Read it and check the main ideas. If you want a copy, click “Descargar PDF”. You can use the summary to review a topic before an exam.

4. Generate an exam

Step by step

  1. First, open “Exámenes”.
  2. Next, click “Generar Examen”.
  3. Then, select a note.
  4. After that, choose the type of question: multiple choice (four options) or true / false.
  5. Then, choose the quantity of questions: 5, 8, 10, 15, or 20.
  6. Finally, click “Generar con IA” and wait.

The AI creates the questions from the content of the selected note. If the system shows an error, click “Intentar de nuevo”.

5. Answer and submit the exam

Step by step

  1. First, read the question carefully.
  2. Next, click one option to select it.
  3. Then, answer all the questions in the same way.
  4. Finally, click “Entregar Examen”.

When you submit the exam, the system grades it automatically. After that, you cannot change your answers.

6. Read your grade

PercentageResultMeaning
70% or moreAprobadoYou passed the exam.
50% to 69%RegularYou need to review some topics.
Less than 50%ReprobadoYou did not pass. Study the note again.

You also see the number of correct and incorrect answers, and an explanation for each answer. The explanation is useful feedback: it tells you why an option is correct or incorrect.

7. Limits and errors

The free AI plan has a quota: a maximum number of requests. If you send many requests in a short time, the AI returns error 429 and the system shows a message like “limit reached”. In that case, wait and try again later. The system does not repeat a 429 request automatically, because every request uses more quota.

Part 4 · Person 4

Chat IA and the Community

This part explains how to talk with the AI tutor, how to send images, and how to ask questions, create polls, answer, and vote in the community.

In this part you will learn

  • How to use the AI chat and send an image.
  • How to search and filter the community.
  • How to ask a question, create a poll, answer, vote, and delete your own post.

1. Chat IA

Chat IA is your AI tutor. You can ask about a topic, request an explanation, or paste a photo of your notes. The AI remembers the context of the conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions.

2. Send a message and send an image

Step by step

  1. First, open “Chat IA”.
  2. Next, write your question in the message box.
  3. Then, press Enter to send it. Use Shift + Enter to start a new line.
  4. After that, read the answer of the AI.
  5. Finally, continue the conversation with a new question.

To send an image, click the image icon and choose a file, or paste an image with Ctrl + V. The maximum size is 5 MB. A preview appears before you send it; click the X to remove it. The AI can analyse images (vision), for example a photo of an exercise.

3. Clear the chat

Click “Limpiar” to delete the conversation and start again. The AI will not remember the old messages.

4. The community feed

Comunidad is a feed of posts from other students. Each card shows the author, the date, a badge (Pregunta or Encuesta), the title, a short preview, and the number of answers or votes. Click a card to open it.

5. Search and filters

Write a word in the search box to find a post. Use the tabs “Todos”, “Preguntas”, and “Encuestas” to filter the feed by type. The list is paginated: it loads in pages, not all at once.

6. Ask a question

Step by step

  1. First, click “Nueva Pregunta”.
  2. Next, write a clear title.
  3. Then, write a description with more details.
  4. After that, add an image if you need it (optional).
  5. Finally, click “Publicar”.

7. Create a poll

Step by step

  1. First, click “Nueva Encuesta”.
  2. Next, write the theme and the question.
  3. Then, add two or more options. Click “Agregar opción” for more options.
  4. After that, check “Solo se puede elegir una opción” if you want one answer per student.
  5. Finally, click “Publicar encuesta”.

8. Answer and vote

Open a question, write your answer in “Tu respuesta”, and click “Publicar respuesta”. Open a poll, select one option (or more, if the poll allows it), and click “Enviar voto”. After you vote, you see the percentage and the bars. You can vote only one time; the system shows “Ya participaste en esta encuesta”.

9. Delete your own post

Step by step

  1. First, click the trash icon at the top of the community.
  2. Next, the system shows only your posts.
  3. Then, click “Eliminar” on the post you want to remove.
  4. Finally, confirm the action. You cannot delete the posts of other students.

Profile, Progress, and Help

This part explains how to manage your profile and photo, how to read your statistics, how to use the mobile app, and what to do when there is a problem.

In this part you will learn

  • How to change your personal data and your photo.
  • How to read your study statistics.
  • How to solve common problems and review the glossary.

1. Your profile

Step by step

  1. First, open “Perfil” in the side menu.
  2. Next, change your name or your email in “Información Personal”.
  3. Then, click “Guardar Cambios”.
  4. Finally, check the message “Cambios guardados”.

2. Change your photo

Click the camera icon on your photo and choose an image from your computer. If you want to remove it, click “Quitar foto”. When you do not have a photo, the system shows your initials.

4. The mobile app

The mobile app uses the same account and the same backend. It has seven tabs: Inicio, Apuntes, Resúmenes, Exámenes, Chat, Comunidad, and Perfil. The app saves your session in a secure place on the phone. In production, the app needs the API address in the variable EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL.

5. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhy it happensWhat should you do?
My account is locked.You wrote a wrong password five times.Wait 10 minutes and try again.
The AI says “limit reached” (429).You used all the AI quota for now.Wait and try later.

I see 404 on another note.The note belongs to another student.Nothing. Notes are private.
My session expired.The system closed the session for security.Log in again.
My summary is empty.The note has little or no content.Write more content and generate again.

7. A recommended daily routine

Step by step

  1. First, log in and open “Inicio”.
  2. Next, open a note or create a new one.
  3. Then, write the main ideas of your class.
  4. After that, generate a summary and read it.
  5. Then, generate a short exam (5 or 8 questions) and answer it.
  6. After that, review the incorrect answers and the explanations.
  7. Then, ask the chat if you still have a doubt.
  8. Finally, check the community and your profile statistics.
