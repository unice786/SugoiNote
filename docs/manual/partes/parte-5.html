SugoiNote Web System · User Manual (English B1)

Profile, Progress, and Help

This part explains how to manage your profile and photo, how to read your statistics, how to use the mobile app, and what to do when there is a problem.

Part 5 of 5 · Person 5
This part is self-contained. It includes learning goals, key vocabulary, step-by-step instructions, review questions, and an answer key.

In this part you will learn

  • How to change your personal data and your photo.
  • How to read your study statistics.
  • How to solve common problems and review the glossary.

1. Your profile

Step by step

  1. First, open “Perfil” in the side menu.
  2. Next, change your name or your email in “Información Personal”.
  3. Then, click “Guardar Cambios”.
  4. Finally, check the message “Cambios guardados”.

2. Change your photo

Click the camera icon on your photo and choose an image from your computer. If you want to remove it, click “Quitar foto”. When you do not have a photo, the system shows your initials.

4. The mobile app

The mobile app uses the same account and the same backend. It has seven tabs: Inicio, Apuntes, Resúmenes, Exámenes, Chat, Comunidad, and Perfil. The app saves your session in a secure place on the phone. In production, the app needs the API address in the variable EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL.

5. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhy it happensWhat should you do?
My account is locked.You wrote a wrong password five times.Wait 10 minutes and try again.
The AI says “limit reached” (429).You used all the AI quota for now.Wait and try later.
My image is not accepted.The image is bigger than 5 MB.Use a smaller image.
My PDF does not open in the editor.The format is not supported.Use PDF, DOCX, HTML, or TXT.
I see 404 on another note.The note belongs to another student.Nothing. Notes are private.
My session expired.The system closed the session for security.Log in again.
My summary is empty.The note has little or no content.Write more content and generate again.

7. A recommended daily routine

Step by step

  1. First, log in and open “Inicio”.
  2. Next, open a note or create a new one.
  3. Then, write the main ideas of your class.
  4. After that, generate a summary and read it.
  5. Then, generate a short exam (5 or 8 questions) and answer it.
  6. After that, review the incorrect answers and the explanations.
  7. Then, ask the chat if you still have a doubt.
  8. Finally, check the community and your profile statistics.

Grammar tip (B1): should for advice

Use should + infinitive to give advice: “You should wait ten minutes.” · “You shouldn’t share your password.”

For a problem, ask: “What should I do if my account is locked?”
