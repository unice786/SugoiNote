Chat IA and the Community

This part explains how to talk with the AI tutor, how to send images, and how to ask questions, create polls, answer, and vote in the community.

Part 4 of 5 · Person 4
This part is self-contained. It includes learning goals, key vocabulary, step-by-step instructions, review questions, and an answer key.

In this part you will learn

  • How to use the AI chat and send an image.
  • How to search and filter the community.
  • How to ask a question, create a poll, answer, vote, and delete your own post.

1. Chat IA

Chat IA is your AI tutor. You can ask about a topic, request an explanation, or paste a photo of your notes. The AI remembers the context of the conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions.

2. Send a message and send an image

Step by step

  1. First, open “Chat IA”.
  2. Next, write your question in the message box.
  3. Then, press Enter to send it. Use Shift + Enter to start a new line.
  4. After that, read the answer of the AI.
  5. Finally, continue the conversation with a new question.

To send an image, click the image icon and choose a file, or paste an image with Ctrl + V. The maximum size is 5 MB. A preview appears before you send it; click the X to remove it. The AI can analyse images (vision), for example a photo of an exercise.

3. Clear the chat

Click “Limpiar” to delete the conversation and start again. The AI will not remember the old messages.

4. The community feed

Comunidad is a feed of posts from other students. Each card shows the author, the date, a badge (Pregunta or Encuesta), the title, a short preview, and the number of answers or votes. Click a card to open it.

5. Search and filters

Write a word in the search box to find a post. Use the tabs “Todos”, “Preguntas”, and “Encuestas” to filter the feed by type. The list is paginated: it loads in pages, not all at once.

6. Ask a question

Step by step

  1. First, click “Nueva Pregunta”.
  2. Next, write a clear title.
  3. Then, write a description with more details.
  4. After that, add an image if you need it (optional).
  5. Finally, click “Publicar”.

7. Create a poll

Step by step

  1. First, click “Nueva Encuesta”.
  2. Next, write the theme and the question.
  3. Then, add two or more options. Click “Agregar opción” for more options.
  4. After that, check “Solo se puede elegir una opción” if you want one answer per student.
  5. Finally, click “Publicar encuesta”.

8. Answer and vote

Open a question, write your answer in “Tu respuesta”, and click “Publicar respuesta”. Open a poll, select one option (or more, if the poll allows it), and click “Enviar voto”. After you vote, you see the percentage and the bars. You can vote only one time; the system shows “Ya participaste en esta encuesta”.

9. Delete your own post

Step by step

  1. First, click the trash icon at the top of the community.
  2. Next, the system shows only your posts.
  3. Then, click “Eliminar” on the post you want to remove.
  4. Finally, confirm the action. You cannot delete the posts of other students.

Key vocabulary

TermSimple definition
ChatA conversation with short messages.
ContextThe information before the current message.
VisionThe ability of the AI to understand images.
FeedA list of posts that updates with new content.
PostA publication in the community.
PollA question with options that students can vote on.
VoteChoose an option in a poll.
ReplyAn answer to a question or a post.
FilterShow only some posts, for example only polls.
PaginationLoad the content in pages.

WH- questions

  • Who can answer a question?
  • When can you delete a post?
  • Why can you vote only one time?
  • How do you attach an image?
  • What is a poll?
  • Where do you write your answer?

Did you…?

  • Did you paste an image in the chat?
  • Did you publish your question?
  • Did you create the poll options?
  • Did you vote in the poll?
  • Did you answer another student?

You should / You shouldn’t

  • ✓ You should write a clear title for your question.
  • ✓ You should be respectful with other students.
  • ✓ You should read the previous answers before you reply.
  • ✕ You shouldn’t share personal data in the community.
  • ✕ You shouldn’t vote two times or write bad words.

Grammar tip (B1): can / can’t for ability and permission

Use can for ability and permission, and can’t for prohibition: “You can paste an image.” · “You can vote only once.” · “You can’t delete another student’s post.”
